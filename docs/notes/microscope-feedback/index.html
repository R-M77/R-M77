How an image becomes a correction

For my master’s research, I developed methods to locate cell features in three dimensions and guide the micromanipulators with visual feedback. A stack of microscope images taken at different depths helps locate the feature. During motion, fresh images let the controller compare the observed positions of the tool and its target and correct the tool’s movement.

Replay the first few seconds and follow the space between the red target and the front of the green box. That gap gives you a visible part of the alignment problem. The paper describes how depth enters the system and reports the experimental results.
